Bible characters, sorted by what they were facing.

Most lists of Bible characters are alphabetical, or by book, or by how important the person was. This one is sorted by what the life was actually about, because that is usually what sends someone looking. Twenty-four of them, with where to read each.

Written by the person who builds Cardinal. Every reference is checked against the Bible text itself.

Find one by what you are carrying

Eight themes, and every life sits under two or three of them. A person who waited was usually also afraid.

Waiting

The twenty-four lives

Each line is the whole arc in one sentence, with the period it traditionally sits in and the books to read it in. Dates before the monarchy are traditional rather than settled.

Abraham

Leaving without a map

c. 2000 BC · Genesis, Hebrews · Waiting, Purpose, Fear

A man who left a settled life on nothing but a voice, and spent the rest of it waiting.

Daniel

Faithful in a place that isn’t home

c. 605 BC · Daniel · Belonging, Fear, Purpose

An exile who kept his window open toward a city that no longer existed.

David

What you do after you can’t undo it

c. 1010 BC · 1 Samuel, 2 Samuel, Psalms · Shame, Mercy, Purpose

A king the record refuses to tidy: the giant, the roof, and what he wrote after.

Elijah

Burned out under a tree

c. 860 BC · 1 Kings · Loneliness, Fear, Purpose

A prophet who won in public and asked to die in private.

Esther

The room you’re afraid to walk into

c. 479 BC · Esther · Fear, Belonging, Purpose

An orphan in hiding who walked into the one room that could kill her.

Gideon

Asking for proof, and getting it

c. 1150 BC · Judges · Fear, Purpose

A man hiding in a winepress, greeted as a mighty warrior.

Hagar

Nobody came looking, and then someone did

c. 2000 BC · Genesis · Loneliness, Mercy

The slave nobody went after, found twice in the desert by God.

Hannah

Praying for what hasn’t come

c. 1100 BC · 1 Samuel · Waiting, Grief

A woman who prayed so honestly the priest thought she was drunk.

Hosea

Loving someone who keeps leaving

c. 750 BC · Hosea · Mercy, Grief

A prophet told to marry someone who would leave, so the country could watch him stay.

Jacob

Getting what you wanted by lying for it

traditionally c. 1900 BC · Genesis · Shame, Belonging

A man who lied for the blessing and wrestled all night to keep it.

Jeremiah

Right, and alone about it

c. 600 BC · Jeremiah · Loneliness, Purpose

A man who spent forty years being right and was never once glad about it.

Job

When the answer never comes

unknown · Job · Grief, Waiting

A man who lost everything and got questions instead of answers.

Jonah

The mercy he didn’t want

c. 780 BC · Jonah, Matthew · Mercy, Fear, Purpose

A prophet who was right about God and hated Him for it.

Joseph

Thirteen years in the wrong room

traditionally c. 1890 BC · Genesis · Waiting, Shame, Mercy

Sold by his brothers, forgotten in prison, and put in charge of the famine.

Martha

Four days too late

c. AD 30 · Luke, John · Grief, Waiting

The practical sister, four days too late and still hoping.

Mary

Yes, before you know what it costs

c. 5 BC · Luke, John · Purpose, Fear, Grief

A girl who said yes first and learned the cost for the rest of her life.

Moses

I’m not the person for this

traditionally c. 1440 BC · Exodus · Fear, Purpose, Belonging

A fugitive with a stammer, sent back to the throne room he fled.

Naomi

Call me bitter

c. 1100 BC · Ruth · Grief, Belonging, Mercy

A woman who came home empty and asked to be renamed for it.

Nehemiah

Rebuilding what everyone else abandoned

c. 445 BC · Nehemiah · Belonging, Purpose

A cupbearer who asked the king for time off and rebuilt a city wall.

Paul

The life you built, undone in three days

c. AD 34 · Acts, Philippians, 2 Corinthians, Galatians · Purpose, Shame, Belonging

The church’s fiercest enemy, stopped on a road and turned into its voice.

Peter

The night you become someone else

c. AD 30 · Matthew, John, Acts · Shame, Mercy, Purpose

The disciple who swore he would never, and then did, and was asked back.

Rahab

A past that doesn’t disqualify you

traditionally c. 1400 BC · Joshua, Matthew, Hebrews · Mercy, Fear, Belonging

A prostitute in a doomed city who bet everything on the God of her enemies.

Ruth

Choosing a life no one planned for you

c. 1100 BC · Ruth, Matthew · Belonging, Waiting, Mercy

A foreign widow whose loyalty wrote her into the family line of kings.

Solomon

Having everything and finding it thin

c. 950 BC · 1 Kings, Ecclesiastes · Purpose, Waiting

The man who was given wisdom, tried everything else, and called it chasing wind.

Why sort them this way

Somebody looking up Esther rarely wants a biography. They want the part where she has to walk into a room that might kill her, because they have a version of that room in front of them on Tuesday. Sorting by name assumes you already know which name you need, which is exactly what you do not know when you are in the middle of something.

So the arc comes first and the person comes second. “The room you are afraid to walk into” leads to Esther. “Burned out under a tree” leads to Elijah. “Four days too late” leads to Martha, who is standing outside a tomb.

This is Threads in Cardinal

In the app each of these is a flyover: the life told as a handful of moments, plotted across a strip of the whole Bible so you can see how far apart they really fall. Abraham’s last moment is in Hebrews, written some two thousand years after the rest of him, and the strip shows that distance rather than tidying it away.

A hundred and forty moments across the twenty-four. Every one carries the verses it rests on, which open into the reader. It is free, it needs no account, and it works with no connection.
